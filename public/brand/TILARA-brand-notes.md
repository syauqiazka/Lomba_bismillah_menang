# TILARA — Logo v1.0

## Concept
A simplified topographic contour symbol with a central point. The contour rings represent overlooked territories; the point represents a need made visible.

## Primary lockup
- `tilara-primary-horizontal.svg`
- Background: Canvas `#F5F6F3`
- Symbol: Forest `#246B50`
- Wordmark: Navy `#172B3A`
- Tagline: Forest `#246B50`

## Reversed lockup
- `tilara-reversed.svg`
- For dark navy backgrounds only.

## Symbol and app icon
- `tilara-symbol.svg`: transparent standalone mark.
- `tilara-app-icon.svg`: square app/avatar tile.

## Brand colors
- Canvas: `#F5F6F3`
- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Navy: `#172B3A`
- Forest: `#246B50`
- Amber: `#E5A13A`
- Alert: `#D85B52`
- Alert Dark: `#B83E37`
- Neutral: `#8A8D86`

## Usage notes
- Keep clear space around the mark at least the diameter of its central point.
- Do not stretch, rotate, outline, or add shadows to the logo.
- Use the standalone symbol only when the TILARA name is already clear in context.
- Do not use Amber or Alert as the primary logo color.
- The tagline is optional at small sizes.

## Status
Exploratory identity v1.0. Before public launch, verify the name through trademark, company-name, and domain checks.
